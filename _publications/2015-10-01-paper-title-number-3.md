We present a semi-Lagrangian characteristic mapping method for incompressible Euler equations on a rotating sphere. The numerical method uses a spatio-temporal discretization of the inverse flow map generated by the Eulerian velocity as a composition of sub-interval flows formed by $C^1$ spherical spline interpolants.
