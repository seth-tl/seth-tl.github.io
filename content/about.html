I am a PIMS Postdoctoral Fellow in the Department of Computer Science at the University of Saskatchewan. My research lies at the intersection of computational mathematics, numerical analysis, and geometric mechanics. I develop and analyze numerical methods for nonlinear partial differential equations and data-driven modelling, with a particular emphasis on the use of geometry in their formulation.

I earned my Ph.D. in the Department of Mathematics and Statistics at McGill University under the supervision of Jean-Christophe Nave. My doctoral research focused on characteristic mapping methods and their generalizations as functional Lie group integrators. I applied these techniques to geophysical PDE models and in the context of geometric operator learning. My current interests include data-driven dynamics, differentiable atmospheric models, and connections between geometry, information theory, and scientific computing.
