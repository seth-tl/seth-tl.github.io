I am a PIMS Postdoctoral Fellow in the Deparment of Computer Science at the University of Saskatchewan. I earned my Ph.D. from McGill University in the Department of Mathematics and Statistics under the supervision of Jean-Christophe Nave. My core expertise is in computational mathematics, numerical analysis, and geometric mechanics. I maintain a wide variety of research interests including information theory, optimal transport, atmospheric dynamics, machine learning, and computational ecology.
